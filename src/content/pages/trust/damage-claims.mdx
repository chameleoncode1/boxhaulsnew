If something is damaged on a BoxHauls haul, photograph it before the driver leaves and report it to BoxHauls. Covered damage is handled under the <F k="INSURANCE_CARRIER" /> policy, which has limits of <F k="COVERAGE_LIMIT" />. The steps are below.

## Photo checklist

- The damage, close up
- The whole item, so the damage is in context
- Any packaging or wrapping
- Where it happened: the doorway, stairs or truck bed
- Your receipt or order, for new items

## What's covered

Coverage, limits and exclusions are on the page about [how your items are insured](/trust/insurance/). For a step-by-step guide to the moment you find damage, see [what to do if an item is damaged](/guides/what-to-do-if-an-item-is-damaged/). For everything else, go back to [trust and safety](/trust/).
