## Refunds

Your card is authorized when you request and charged only when the driver marks the trip complete. If you cancel for a full refund, the authorization is released, so nothing is charged; your bank decides how fast the hold disappears. If the cancellation fee applies, only <F k="CANCEL_FEE" /> of the quoted price is charged.
