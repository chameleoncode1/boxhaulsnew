A BoxHauls trip takes seven steps, and the price is fixed at step three, before you book. You enter two addresses and say what's moving. You see a price built from one formula, and a local driver with a pickup does the hauling.

## The price comes before the booking

Every trip uses the same formula: <F k="BASE_FARE" /> base plus <F k="PER_MILE" /> a mile. Two add-ons can apply: <F k="HEAVY_FEE" /> for heavy items (<F k="HEAVY_FEE_ITEMS" />) and <F k="HELPER_FEE" /> for a second person. There's no hourly rate and no surge. For the numbers by distance, see [what a truck and driver costs](/pricing/).

## The three job types

- **<F k="TIER_1" />**: boxes and small-move loads.
- **<F k="TIER_2" />**: junk headed to the landfill or transfer station. Dump fees are passed through at cost.
- **<F k="TIER_3" />**: furniture, appliances and other single large items.

The job type tells the driver what to expect. It doesn't change the price formula. If you're not sure your item fits in a pickup bed, check [which job type and truck fit your stuff](/pricing/truck-sizes/).

## Helpers

Some drivers bring a second person. If you request a helper, it adds <F k="HELPER_FEE" />, and only a driver who has one can accept your trip. Helpers aren't guaranteed. For two-person items like refrigerators and washers, have someone ready to help load in case no driver with a helper is available.

## Who's driving

Drivers use their own pickup trucks and are screened by <F k="BACKGROUND_PROVIDER" /> before their first trip. Every haul is insured by <F k="INSURANCE_CARRIER" />. Here's [how BoxHauls vets drivers and insures your items](/trust/).
