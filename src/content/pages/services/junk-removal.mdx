Junk removal with BoxHauls is priced by the truckload. That's <F k="BASE_FARE" /> plus <F k="PER_MILE" /> a mile plus the dump fee at cost, in <F k="METRO" /> and <F k="NEIGHBORHOOD" />. A driver with a pickup loads it and takes it to the landfill or transfer station. Book it as <F k="TIER_2" />.

## Per-load pricing

Every dump run uses the same formula. The disposal site's gate fee is passed through at cost, with no markup. For the numbers and what changes them, see [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/).
