## Upright only

Laying a refrigerator on its side can let compressor oil into the cooling lines. BoxHauls moves refrigerators upright, every time.

## Defrost 24 hours ahead

Empty it, unplug it and leave the doors open about 24 hours before pickup so the freezer defrosts and drains. Wipe it dry, then tape the doors shut for the ride.
