## Shipping bolts

Front-load washers need their shipping bolts installed before a move. The bolts lock the drum so it can't swing and damage the suspension. Check your manual for where they go.

## Stacking kits

Stacked pairs ride unstacked, side by side. Keep the stacking kit's hardware in a bag taped to the dryer.

## Dryer vents

Disconnect the vent duct and leave it behind if it's crushed or lint-clogged. A new 4-inch foil or semi-rigid duct is cheap and safer. Gas dryers must be disconnected and capped by a licensed professional before the driver arrives.
