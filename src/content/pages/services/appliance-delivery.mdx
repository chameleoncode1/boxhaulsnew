Old appliances can be hauled away for recycling.

## Two people, standard

Full-size appliances weigh 150–350 lb, so they're two-person jobs. Request a helper for <F k="HELPER_FEE" />. Helpers come with the driver and aren't guaranteed, so if no driver with a helper accepts, have a second person ready to help load and unload.

## Prep checklist

- **Refrigerators and freezers.** Empty, unplug and defrost about 24 hours ahead. Tape the doors shut.
- **Washers.** Drain the hoses. Install shipping bolts on front-loaders.
- **Dryers.** Coil the cord. Have a licensed professional disconnect and cap a gas line.
- **All appliances.** Measure the doorways at both ends. Most washers and dryers are about 27 inches wide, and refrigerators 28–36 inches.

## Stairs and doorways

There's no stairs fee, but appliances on stairs always take two people. Removing refrigerator doors can buy 2–4 inches in a tight doorway.
