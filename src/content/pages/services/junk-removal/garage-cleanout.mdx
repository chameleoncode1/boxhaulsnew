## Sort before the truck comes

1. **Keep.** Back on the shelf.
2. **Donate or sell.** Box it separately.
3. **Junk.** Pile it near the garage door.
4. **Hazardous.** Set it aside; it can't ride.

Sorting first means the driver loads only junk, and you pay for fewer loads.

## Hazardous materials

Paint, solvents, pesticides, motor oil, fuel, propane tanks and car batteries can't go in a BoxHauls truck or into the landfill. Take them to a household hazardous waste drop-off. <Todo>Fresno-area household hazardous waste site</Todo>
