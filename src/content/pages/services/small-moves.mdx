## Per trip vs. hourly

Movers usually bill by the hour, often with a two- or three-hour minimum, so a slow elevator or a long carry costs you. BoxHauls charges for the trip: base fare plus miles. You see the price before you book, and loading time doesn't change it.

## What fits in one pickup load

Book small moves as <F k="TIER_1" />. A standard 6.5 ft bed holds roughly 55–60 cubic feet up to the bed rails, more when strapped and stacked higher. Rough capacity per load:

| Load | Fits in one trip |
|---|---|
| Boxes only | About 20–30 medium boxes, stacked and strapped |
| Studio furniture | A bed, a dresser and a few boxes, or a couch and a table |
| Mixed | A couch plus 10–15 boxes |

These are estimates. Furniture shape matters more than box count.

## Helpers

Small moves almost always need two people for the bed and couch. Request a helper for <F k="HELPER_FEE" />; helpers come with the driver and aren't guaranteed, so have a friend on standby.
