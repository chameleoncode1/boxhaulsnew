## Who uses it

- **Store buyers.** You bought it in the store, and it doesn't fit in your car. [Store pickup and delivery](/services/furniture-delivery/store-pickup/) covers Costco, Living Spaces, Home Depot, Lowe's and more.
- **Marketplace buyers.** You found it on Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist or OfferUp. See [Marketplace pickup with a truck](/services/furniture-delivery/marketplace-pickup/).

## Wrapping and protection

An open truck bed means wind, dust and the occasional <F k="METRO" /> summer dust storm. Drivers wrap upholstered furniture and wood finishes in moving blankets and strap every piece so it can't shift. Bag mattresses before pickup; see [mattress delivery](/services/furniture-delivery/mattress-delivery/).
