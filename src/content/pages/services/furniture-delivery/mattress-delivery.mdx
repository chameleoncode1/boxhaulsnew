## How it rides

| Size | Width × length (in) | In a 6.5 ft bed |
|---|---|---|
| Twin, twin XL | 38 × 75–80 | Flat |
| Full | 54 × 75 | Flat |
| Queen | 60 × 80 | Nearly flat, over the wheel wells |
| King, California king | 72–76 × 80–84 | Angled or on edge |

Book mattresses as <F k="TIER_3" />. There's no heavy-item fee for mattresses.

## Box spring and frame

The price is per trip, not per piece. Take the frame apart before pickup and bag the bolts.
