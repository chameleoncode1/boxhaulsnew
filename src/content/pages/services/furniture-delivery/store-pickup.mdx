Store pickup with BoxHauls means a driver with a pickup collects what you bought and brings it home. That works for furniture, appliances, lumber, or anything too big for your car. It costs the standard BoxHauls formula: <F k="BASE_FARE" /> plus <F k="PER_MILE" /> a mile. It's one of the [furniture and large-item delivery](/services/furniture-delivery/) services.

## How store pickup works

1. Buy the item and choose in-store pickup.
2. Book BoxHauls with the store as the pickup address and your home as the drop-off.
3. Share your order number and the name on the order.
4. The driver goes to the store's pickup area, loads, straps and drives it to you.
