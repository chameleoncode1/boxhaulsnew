## Items that need a heads-up

These are fine, but tell us when you book:

- **Heavy appliances.** <F k="HEAVY_FEE_ITEMS" /> get the <F k="HEAVY_FEE" /> heavy-item fee and usually need two people.
- **Glass tabletops and mirrors.** Wrap them, or leave them in the original box.
- **Gas appliances.** Have a licensed professional disconnect and cap the gas line first.
