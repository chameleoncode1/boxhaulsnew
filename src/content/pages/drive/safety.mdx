Hauling is physical work, so safety comes first. Lift heavy items with two people and a dolly, meet in visible places, plan around <F k="METRO" />'s summer heat, and turn down anything outside BoxHauls' limits.

## Lifting

- Two people for anything over about 100 lb, and every refrigerator, washer and sleeper sofa.
- Use an appliance dolly for appliances and safes.
- Lift with your legs, keep the load close, and don't twist while carrying.
- Plan the path first: measure doorways, clear the route, and note the stairs.

## Meeting-point safety

- Park legally and in view.
- Confirm the address and the customer's name before loading.
- If a pickup feels wrong, leave and contact support. <Todo>driver safety support line and in-app reporting</Todo>

## Heat

From June through September, <F k="METRO" /> regularly tops 100°F. Carry water, take morning jobs when you can, rest in shade between loads, and know the signs of heat exhaustion: dizziness, nausea, heavy sweating.

## Weather and the road

Tarp loads when rain is possible. Strap everything, and re-check straps after the first mile and at every stop. Secure loose items so nothing blows out of the bed on the freeway.

- Single items over <F k="MAX_ITEM_WEIGHT" /> lb, or gun safes over <F k="MAX_SAFE_WEIGHT" /> lb
- Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks and other hazardous materials
- Live animals
- Anything you can't secure in your truck
