Apply to drive for BoxHauls in <F k="METRO" /> and <F k="NEIGHBORHOOD" />. You'll need a pickup truck, a valid license, auto insurance and a smartphone, and you'll go through a background check with <F k="BACKGROUND_PROVIDER" />.

## Apply

Apply in the BoxHauls Driver app: [create your driver account at driver.boxhauls.com](https://driver.boxhauls.com/). It installs on any phone from your browser, and the same app is where you go online and take jobs once you're approved.

## What happens next

1. Create your driver account and fill in your details.
2. Complete the background check with <F k="BACKGROUND_PROVIDER" />.
3. Add your truck and upload photos of your driver's license, registration and insurance card in the app. BoxHauls reviews them.
4. Set up payouts with Stripe and accept the driver agreement, then submit your application.
5. Go online and accept your first job.
