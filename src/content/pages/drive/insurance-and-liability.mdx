## What personal auto policies exclude

Many personal auto policies exclude "livery" or business use, meaning driving for pay. If you haul for BoxHauls on a personal policy that excludes it, a claim from a hauling trip could be denied. Call your insurer before your first trip.

## Commercial options

- **Business-use or delivery endorsement.** Added to your personal policy; usually the cheapest fix.
- **Commercial auto policy.** For drivers hauling most days.
- **Inland marine or cargo coverage.** Covers goods in your care beyond the platform's limits.

Ask your insurer which fits.
