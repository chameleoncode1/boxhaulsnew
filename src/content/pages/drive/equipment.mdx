Every hauling driver should carry ratchet straps, moving blankets, an appliance dolly, a tarp and work gloves. A starter kit runs roughly $250–$600. The full list is below, with rough costs.

## The kit

| Gear | How many | Why | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ratchet straps (1 in, 10–15 ft) | 4–8 | Every load gets strapped | $20–$50 for a 4-pack |
| Moving blankets | 6–12 | Protect finishes and upholstery | $40–$90 a dozen |
| Appliance dolly with strap | 1 | Refrigerators, washers, safes | $100–$250 |
| Furniture dolly (4-wheel) | 1 | Dressers, boxes, long carries | $15–$40 |
| Tarp (8 × 10 ft or larger) | 1–2 | Rain and dust in an open bed | $15–$40 |
| Work gloves | 2 pairs | Grip and splinters | $10–$25 |
| Bungee cords or a bed net | 1 set | Loose small items | $15–$35 |
| Furniture sliders | 1 set | Moving across floors without scratching | $10–$20 |
| Ramps | Optional | Very heavy wheeled items | $100–$300 |

Prices are rough retail ranges.

## Tie-down basics

Strap tall items like refrigerators to the front of the bed, upright. Put heavy items low and forward. Run straps over the item and anchor to the bed's tie-down points, not the tailgate. Check every strap after the first mile.
