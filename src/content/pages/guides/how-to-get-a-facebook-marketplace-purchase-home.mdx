To pick up furniture from Facebook Marketplace safely, measure first, meet in daylight, inspect before you pay, and have a truck and a second person lined up before you go. Here's the full checklist.

## Before you agree to buy

1. **Get dimensions.** Ask the seller for length, depth and height, or measure from the listing.
2. **Measure your doorways and stairs.**
3. **Ask about the pickup.** Stairs? Elevator? Ground floor? Where can a truck park?
4. **Agree on a time** in daylight.

## Before you go

- Tell someone where you're going.
- Line up a truck and a second person. If you're hiring a truck and driver, book it before you confirm the time with the seller.
- Bring moving blankets, straps and a tape measure.

## At the seller's

- Inspect before paying. See the checklist below.
- Pay only after you've seen it, using a method that leaves a record. Never send a deposit for an item you haven't seen.
- Don't go inside alone if you're uncomfortable. Ask the seller to bring it to the garage or curb.

## Inspection checklist

| Check | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Bed bugs | Seams, joints and under cushions: dark spots, shed skins |
| Frame | Wobble, cracks, broken joints |
| Drawers and doors | Open and close every one |
| Upholstery | Stains, tears, smoke or pet smell |
| Mechanisms | Recliners and sleepers: run them fully |

## If the seller no-shows

Message them, wait 15 minutes, and leave. Don't send money to hold a new time.
