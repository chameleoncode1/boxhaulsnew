Fresno has two main places to dump a load. At the American Avenue Disposal Site (the Fresno County landfill near Kerman), general refuse is $29.52 a ton, and loads up to 640 lb are a flat $9.45. At the Cedar Avenue Recycling and Transfer Station (CARTS) in Fresno, most junk is $90 a ton, plus $16 environmental and $10 fuel fees on every load.

## What a pickup load costs

A pickup bed of household junk usually weighs a few hundred pounds to half a ton. At CARTS that's roughly $37–$71 with the per-load fees. At American Avenue the same load is $9.45–$15, but it's a longer drive west. Heavy materials like dirt, concrete and construction debris weigh much more for the same space.

## Which one to use

CARTS is the shorter drive from most of Fresno and Clovis and takes more kinds of material, including free drop-off for clean metal, clean concrete or asphalt, and up to 10 mattresses a day. American Avenue is the county landfill, a longer drive west, with much lower per-ton rates for general refuse and yard waste. Cover your load: American Avenue charges $8 for uncovered loads.
