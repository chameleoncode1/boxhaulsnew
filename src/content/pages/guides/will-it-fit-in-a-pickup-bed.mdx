Most household items fit in a full-size pickup bed. A standard 6.5 ft bed takes a three-seat couch flat, a queen mattress nearly flat, and a refrigerator standing up. A king mattress rides angled, and anything over about 8 feet needs a long bed or the tailgate down. Here are dimensions and fit for 40 common items.

## Pickup bed sizes

| Bed | Length (in) | Between wheel wells (in) | Widest point (in) | Side height (in) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short (5.5 ft) | 66–67 | 48–51 | 62–66 | 20–22 |
| Standard (6.5 ft) | 78–79 | 48–51 | 62–66 | 20–22 |
| Long (8 ft) | 96–98 | 48–51 | 62–66 | 20–22 |

Typical full-size pickup ranges. The tailgate adds roughly 18–22 inches when it's down.

## Reading the table

- **Over wells** means it rides on top of the wheel wells, where the bed is 62–66 inches wide.
- **Tailgate down** means it rides with the tailgate open and the load strapped so it can't slide.
- **Angled** means it's too wide to lie flat, so it rides on edge or tilted against the bed side.
- **Overhang**: flag any load that sticks out 4 feet or more past the tailgate.
