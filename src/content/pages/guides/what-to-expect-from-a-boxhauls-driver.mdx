A BoxHauls driver is a screened local with their own pickup truck. They load your item, wrap and strap it, drive it to the drop-off, and unload it. They bring moving blankets and straps. If you requested a helper and the driver brought one, there'll be two people. Here's how a trip usually goes.

## Before the driver arrives

- Measure the item and the doorways at both ends.
- Clear a path, and know where the truck can park.
- Have the item ready: cushions off, drawers emptied or taped, appliances unplugged and drained.

## At pickup

The driver confirms the item, checks the path, and decides how to carry it. Upholstery and wood finishes get wrapped in moving blankets. Every item is strapped in the bed so it can't shift.

## Helpers

Heavy items take two people. If you requested a helper, only a driver bringing one can accept your trip. Helpers aren't guaranteed, so for two-person items, have someone ready just in case.

## Tipping

Tips are optional and go to your driver.
