For one or two items across town, hiring a truck and driver is often cheaper than renting a truck. A rental adds a base fee, per-mile charges, fuel, an optional damage waiver, and two trips to the rental counter, and you still do the lifting. For a full apartment, renting usually wins. ## The hidden miles

A 10-mile move in a rental is rarely 10 miles. You drive to the counter, take the truck to the pickup, go 10 miles to the drop-off, then return the truck and drive home. That's often 30–50 miles of driving, and a few hours.

## When renting wins

- You're moving a full apartment or house
- You have help and a free afternoon
- You need the truck for several stops

## When hiring wins

- One or two big items
- No one to help lift
- You don't want to drive a truck you've never driven
