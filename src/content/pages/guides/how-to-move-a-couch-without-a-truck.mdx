To move a couch without a truck, hire a truck and driver, rent a truck, use the store's delivery, or borrow a pickup. For one couch across town, hiring a truck and driver is usually fastest, and often cheapest once you count rental fees, mileage and gas. Here's how to do it either way without damaging the couch or your doorways.

## Your options

| Option | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Hire a truck and driver | One couch, same day, no lifting alone | Helpers aren't always included; ask |
| Rent a pickup or van | When you have two people and time | Rental fee, mileage, fuel, and you do the work |
| Store delivery | Brand-new couches | Delivery windows can be days out |
| Borrow a truck | A friend with a pickup and an afternoon | No insurance on the load |
| Roof rack | Don't | Couches are too wide and catch wind |

## Steps

Follow the steps below whichever way the couch travels. The measuring step prevents most failed moves.
