## What BoxHauls is

## What BoxHauls isn't

- BoxHauls is not freight shipping. Nothing ships across state lines, and there are no shared multi-stop loads.
- It isn't a long-distance mover. Trips stay within <F k="RADIUS" /> miles of <F k="METRO" />.
- It isn't dumpster rental. A driver hauls your junk away the same trip.
- It isn't rideshare. The truck carries your things, not passengers.
- It isn't a truck rental company. You don't drive; a driver does.
- It isn't a labor-only crew. Every booking includes a truck.
