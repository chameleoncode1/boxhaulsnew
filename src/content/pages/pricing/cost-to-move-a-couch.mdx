## Helper guidance

Most couches take two people. A driver can't safely carry a 150-lb sofa down a staircase alone. If you request a helper, only a driver who brings one can accept the job, and helpers aren't guaranteed. If you don't request one, plan to lift one end yourself or have a friend there.

## How to prep a couch

1. Measure the couch and every doorway on the way out. Most interior doors are 30–36 inches wide.
2. Take off loose cushions and pillows and bag them.
3. Remove legs that unscrew. Most do, and it saves an inch or two.
4. Clear a path to the door and note any tight turns.
5. For a sleeper sofa, close and secure the mechanism so it can't open in transit.
