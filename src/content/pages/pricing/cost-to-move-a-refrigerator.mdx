## Helper guidance

Every full-size refrigerator is a two-person job. Request a helper, but helpers come with the driver and aren't guaranteed. If no driver with a helper accepts, you'll need a second person ready to help load and unload.

## How to prep a refrigerator

1. Empty it and unplug it about 24 hours before the move, so the freezer defrosts and drains.
2. Remove shelves and drawers, or tape them in place.
3. Shut off and disconnect the water line if there's an ice maker. <Todo>confirm drivers don't disconnect or connect water lines</Todo>
4. Tape the doors closed and coil the cord to the back.
5. Measure the doorways at both ends. Removing the doors can buy 2–4 inches.
6. Check the manual for how long to wait before plugging it back in. In <F k="METRO" /> summer heat, have its new spot ready so it doesn't sit in a hot garage or driveway.
