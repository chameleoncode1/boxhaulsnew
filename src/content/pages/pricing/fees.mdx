BoxHauls has five possible line items. Every trip has the first two, and the other three apply only when they're triggered. Nothing else is added.

| Line item | When it applies | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Base fare | Every trip | <F k="BASE_FARE" /> |
| Per mile | Every trip, for the trip distance | <F k="PER_MILE" /> per mile |
| Heavy-item fee | The trip includes <F k="HEAVY_FEE_ITEMS" /> | <F k="HEAVY_FEE" /> per trip, however many heavy items |
| Dump fee | Dump runs: the landfill or transfer station's fee | At cost, no markup |

## What's never charged

- No hourly rate. You pay by the trip.
- No surge pricing.
- No minimum fare.
- No stairs fee.
- No wait-time fee.
- No fuel surcharge.

## Tips

Tips are optional and go to your driver.

## Dump fees

On a dump run, the driver pays the gate fee at the landfill or transfer station, and that fee is passed through to you at cost. On a dump run, you tell us what's in the load and roughly how much. We add the most the dump fee could be for that load to your card hold, and you're charged only the actual fee from the dump's receipt. See [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for the holds by load.
