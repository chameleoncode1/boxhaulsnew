## What's never charged

- No hourly rate. You pay by the trip.
- No surge pricing.
- No minimum fare.
- No stairs fee.
- No wait-time fee.
- No fuel surcharge.

## Tips

Tips are optional and go to your driver.

On a dump run, the driver pays the gate fee at the landfill or transfer station, and that fee is passed through to you at cost. <Todo>how the dump fee is shown and charged: on the receipt after the trip, or estimated before booking</Todo>
