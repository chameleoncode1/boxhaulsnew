## What changes the price

- **Distance.** Every mile adds <F k="PER_MILE" />.
- **Not one machine or two.** The heavy-item fee is per trip, so moving both costs the same as moving one.
- **Not the stairs.** There's no stairs fee.

## Lifting

Each machine is a two-person carry. Have a second person ready at both ends to lift with the driver.

## How to prep a washer and dryer

1. Run the washer empty, then drain the hoses into a bucket. Leftover water leaks in transit.
2. Install the shipping bolts on a front-loader. Check your manual for where they go.
3. Shut off the water and disconnect the hoses. Bag them with the bolts and tape the bag to the machine.
4. For a gas dryer, have the gas line disconnected and capped by a licensed professional before the driver arrives.
5. Unstack a stacked pair.
6. Measure the laundry-room doorway. Washers and dryers are about 27 inches wide.
