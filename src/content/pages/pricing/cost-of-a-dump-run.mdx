## Load sizes

The trip price doesn't change with load size. The dump fee does, because disposal sites charge by weight or volume.

| Load | Roughly | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Quarter bed | A few bags and one piece of furniture | An old recliner and yard bags |
| Half bed | A room's worth of junk | Garage shelving, boxes, a broken grill |
| Full bed | Level with the bed sides | A garage cleanout or a full room |

## What changes the price

- **Distance to the site.** Every mile adds <F k="PER_MILE" />.
- **The dump fee.** Set by the site, passed through at cost.
- **A helper.** Adds <F k="HELPER_FEE" /> if you want help loading.
- **Heavy items.** Refrigerators, freezers, washers and dryers get the <F k="HEAVY_FEE" /> heavy-item fee.
