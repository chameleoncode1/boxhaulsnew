## What changes the price

- **Distance.** Every mile adds <F k="PER_MILE" />.
- **A helper.** Adds <F k="HELPER_FEE" />.
- **Not the size.** Twin and king cost the same to move.
- **Not the extra pieces.** Box spring, frame and headboard ride on the same trip.

## Helper guidance

A twin or full is a one-person carry for most drivers. Queens and kings flop and catch on door frames, so a helper or a second person on your end makes the job safer. Helpers come with the driver and aren't guaranteed.

## How to prep a mattress

1. Strip the bedding.
2. Put the mattress in a plastic mattress bag and tape it closed. In an open truck bed, a bag is the difference between clean and not.
3. Take the frame apart and bag the bolts. Tape the bag to the headboard.
4. Clear the hallway and stairs.
