BoxHauls job types describe the job, not the truck. Book <F k="TIER_1" /> for boxes and small moves, <F k="TIER_2" /> for junk and <F k="TIER_3" /> for furniture and appliances. Every driver uses their own pickup. A standard 6.5 ft bed takes a full-size couch, a queen mattress flat, or a refrigerator standing up.

## Which job type to book

| Job type | Book it for | Typical load |
|---|---|---|
| <F k="TIER_1" /> | Boxes and small-move loads | Studio or dorm boxes, a few small pieces |
| <F k="TIER_2" /> | Junk and dump runs | Old furniture, yard waste, garage cleanout |
| <F k="TIER_3" /> | One or a few large items | Couch, mattress, refrigerator, washer and dryer |

All three use the same formula: <F k="BASE_FARE" /> plus <F k="PER_MILE" /> a mile. See [BoxHauls pricing](/pricing/) for the numbers.

## Pickup bed sizes

| Bed | Length (in) | Width between wheel wells (in) | Side height (in) | Typical trucks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short (5.5 ft) | 66–67 | 48–51 | 20–22 | Crew-cab half-tons |
| Standard (6.5 ft) | 78–79 | 48–51 | 20–22 | Regular and extended-cab half-tons |
| Long (8 ft) | 96–98 | 48–51 | 20–22 | Three-quarter-ton and work trucks |

Measurements are typical manufacturer ranges for full-size pickups. Lowering the tailgate adds roughly 18–22 inches of support.

## Payload

A half-ton pickup carries roughly 1,500–2,300 lb; a three-quarter-ton carries roughly 2,500–4,000 lb, depending on configuration. BoxHauls caps single items at <F k="MAX_ITEM_WEIGHT" /> lb and gun safes at <F k="MAX_SAFE_WEIGHT" /> lb.

## What fits, and what doesn't

| Item | Fits a 6.5 ft bed? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Three-seat sofa (72–90 in) | Yes | Longer sofas ride with the tailgate down |
| Sectional | In pieces | Most separate into 2–5 sections |
| Queen mattress (60 × 80 in) | Yes, nearly flat | Rides over the wheel wells |
| King mattress (76 × 80 in) | Angled | Too wide to lie flat between the sides |
| Refrigerator | Yes, upright | Strapped to the front of the bed |
| Washer or dryer | Yes | About 27 in wide each; both fit together |
| 4 × 8 ft plywood | Short beds no, long beds yes | Rides on the wheel wells with the tailgate down in shorter beds |
| Grand piano | No | Needs a piano specialist |
